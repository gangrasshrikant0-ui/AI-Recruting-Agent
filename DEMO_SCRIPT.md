# 5-minute guided demo

1. **Create job (0:00–0:35)**  
   Click **Create job** in the Guided Demo banner. Paste or keep the seeded JD and click **Analyze with AI**. Point out the generated criteria, weights, and interview blueprint.

2. **Matching (0:35–1:00)**  
   Click **Matching**. Explain that 25 candidates are seeded across three jobs and 16 ATS stages, with duplicate/conflict and stuck-candidate indicators.

3. **Approve shortlist (1:00–1:30)**  
   Click **Approve shortlist**, approve Maya’s shortlist request, and note the candidate notification written to Outbox. This demonstrates human-in-the-loop control.

4. **Consent + schedule (1:30–2:00)**  
   Click **Consent + schedule**. Show candidate/interviewer overlap, timezone, meeting link, `.ics` download, reschedule alternatives, cancellation, and attendance states.

5. **Interview (2:00–2:45)**  
   Click **Interview**, use the consent screen, then click **Demo interview · 6 turns**. Highlight editable transcript, low-confidence segments, adaptive follow-ups, no-repeat logic, and reload-safe state.

6. **Evaluation + report (2:45–3:45)**  
   Click **Evaluation + report**. Select Maya, then Jon or Sara. Show:
   - `Grounded ✓` vs `Flagged ⚠`
   - Turn-indexed transcript quotes
   - demonstrated-vs-claimed
   - insufficient evidence instead of guessed scores
   - fairness redaction and human review status

7. **Decision (3:45–4:25)**  
   Click **Decision**, drag a candidate to a high-impact stage, and show that the move creates an approval instead of silently changing the ATS. Approve an offer in **Approvals inbox**.

8. **Analytics and audit (4:25–5:00)**  
   Click **Analytics** for real derived KPIs, funnel, timing, attendance, bottlenecks, competency distribution, fairness sample warning, and aggregate predictive insights. Finish on **Audit trail** to show the human and AI actions and agent prompt versions.

If the demo state gets messy, click **Reset demo data** in the Guided Demo banner.
